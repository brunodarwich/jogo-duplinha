# Heróis chibi

Gerados com a ferramenta integrada image_gen. Originais preservados em `originals/`.

Prompt base: Use case: stylized-concept. Create one production-quality fantasy MOBA character sprite, isolated on actual transparent background, square canvas. Extremely squat super-deformed CHIBI: EXACTLY 1.5 HEADS TALL. Anatomical head crown-to-chin occupies TWO THIRDS of crown-to-soles height; entire body below chin including tiny stubby feet only ONE THIRD. Head twice as tall as body and legs combined. No visible neck, tiny mitten hands, almost no legs. This ratio is the highest priority. Clean polished hand-painted 2D game illustration with bold dark silhouette outlines, simple cel-shaded volumes, adorable expressive face, readable at 78 pixels. Full body, centered, three-quarter front view facing slightly right, slight elevated isometric camera. Feet near bottom at 90% canvas height, all equipment fully in frame, small consistent margin. No scenery, pedestal, ground shadow, text, labels, watermark, borders, multiple views or other characters. 

Classes: cavaleiro azul/prata/dourado com espada e escudo; maga vermelha com cabelo cobre, cajado de fogo e cristal de gelo; arqueiro verde com arco; assassina violeta com adagas verdes; clérigo branco/dourado com cajado solar e livro.

A maga foi refeita usando o novo cavaleiro como referência visual direta: mesma ilustração 2D, contornos escuros, sombras facetadas, cabeça enorme e corpo minúsculo, sem cenário nem acabamento de boneca 3D.
